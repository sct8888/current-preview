Privacy is the brand

If we can see your log, we built it wrong.

  • No account to start.
  • On-device first. Optional encrypted iCloud you control.
  • HealthKit reads sleep and workouts. Intimate entries never written back in v1.
  • No SDKs that monetise “custom events.” That lesson already got paid.
