The gap

Sex sells because men already think in streaks. They just hide the ledger.

Every women’s health unicorn started as a calendar people were slightly embarrassed to open - then couldn’t stop opening. Men already count. They just do it in their head, badly.

Current is not another testosterone-optimisation coach. It is not a body-count trophy case. It is the missing male half of the couple’s intimate calendar: when, how you felt, what the week around it looked like.

“She can tell you she’s ovulating on Thursday. You can tell her ‘I think I’m tired.’ That’s the product.”

The Flo move

A monthly ritual plus a sex mark. We keep the ritual. We drop the pink UI and the partner-nudge copy.

Why fitness is in the room

Not because the App Store is full of gym apps. Because sleep debt and overload are the two things that actually move drive. They earn their place as side-channels.

What we will not ship

Leaderboards. Partner surveillance. “You should be having sex every 72 hours.” Ads. A community tab.

Privacy is the brand

If we can see your log, we built it wrong.

  • No account to start.
  • On-device first. Optional encrypted iCloud you control.
  • HealthKit reads sleep and workouts. Intimate entries never written back in v1.
  • No SDKs that monetise “custom events.” Flo already paid for that lesson.

Private beta. Cape Town first.

Thirty men. Thirty days. If they still open it in week four, we build the real app.
